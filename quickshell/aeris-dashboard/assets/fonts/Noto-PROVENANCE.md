# Clock companion font

`NotoSans-CondensedBold.ttf` is the unmodified upright Condensed Bold font
bundled with Fedora's `google-noto-sans-fonts-20251201-2.fc44.noarch` package.
Its internal font version is 2.015. Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors;
licensed under SIL OFL 1.1 (see `Noto-OFL.txt`).

Used only as the clock's companion glyph mask. The original Iosevka Bold
remains unchanged in the other two RGB masks and determines the clock layout.
Each companion digit's ink bounds are fitted to the corresponding Iosevka
digit before applying small offsets. No italics or full-string stretching.
